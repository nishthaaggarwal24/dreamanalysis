## Architecture & Landing Page Narrative

The root URL (`http://localhost:8501`) features a world-class **10-Section AI Product Landing Page** following strict SaaS storytelling flow (inspired by Stripe, Linear, Notion, Apple, and Vercel):

1. **SECTION 1 — HERO**: Full viewport hero with headline *"Understand the patterns hidden inside your dreams"*, zero charts above the fold, primary CTAs (`Start Your Dream Journey ->`, `Explore Collective Intelligence ->`), and a capability navigation strip (`Dream Analysis • Digital Twin • Discoveries • Storylines • Evolution • Reports`).
2. **SECTION 2 — PROBLEM + SOLUTION**: Two-column layout with problem statement *"Why do the same dreams keep returning?"* and simplified pipeline flow (`Dream -> Pattern -> Discovery -> Digital Twin`).
3. **SECTION 3 — PLATFORM CAPABILITIES**: 3 Pillar product cards (**Dream Intelligence**, **Digital Twin**, **Collective Intelligence**).
4. **SECTION 4 — HOW IT WORKS**: Dark full-width 5-step timeline (`01 Record -> 02 Extract -> 03 Emerge -> 04 Evolve -> 05 Discover`).
5. **SECTION 5 — DISCOVERIES**: Real discovery cards (*"Family appears in 71% of positive dreams"*, *"Water symbolism increased before transitions"*).
6. **SECTION 6 — DIGITAL TWIN SHOWCASE**: *"Meet your subconscious twin"* split layout with Dream DNA & Current Season preview.
7. **SECTION 7 — ORGANIZATION MODE**: *"What is society dreaming about?"* societal emotional climate observatory section.
8. **SECTION 8 — PLATFORM ECOSYSTEM**: 10-tile ecosystem capability grid.
9. **SECTION 9 — WHY IT'S DIFFERENT**: Direct comparison table (Traditional Dream Journal vs Dream Intelligence Platform).
10. **SECTION 10 — FINAL CTA**: High-converting closing headline and primary conversion actions.
